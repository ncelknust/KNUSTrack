## What is KNUSTrack?

KNUSTrack is a carbon-intelligence platform for **KNUST's College of Engineering**. It turns
the activity data the College already keeps — procurement spend, metered electricity, fuel
and refrigerant records, and a campus commuting survey — into a complete, auditable
greenhouse-gas inventory across **Scopes 1, 2 and 3**, and presents it as an interactive
dashboard with a costed pathway to **net zero by 2040**.

This is **Phase 1 of the College of Engineering** — the first faculty to be fully assessed.
The same platform is designed to extend to the wider University in later phases as each
part of KNUST is measured.

---

## What you can do with it

📊 **See the whole footprint.** The College's complete 2025 baseline — 1,927 tCO₂e — with
each scope's size and share, and the key insights behind the numbers.

🔍 **Break it down by scope.** Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (electricity) and Scope 3 (indirect),
each resolved to its underlying sources with clear, transparent methodology.

🏢 **Compare buildings and departments.** A device-level energy audit across eleven buildings
and a department-by-department ranking show exactly where emissions concentrate.

🚌 **Understand commuting.** Staff and student travel modelled from a survey of 1,115 students —
mode share, distance bands, and the decarbonisation options that follow.

📈 **Track trends and methods.** Three years of supply-chain emissions (2023–2025) under three
emission-factor methods (EEIO, DEFRA, Hybrid) for full transparency.

🎯 **Follow the net-zero pathway.** A costed trajectory to 2040 — priority interventions plus
residual-emission offsets through Enhanced Rock Weathering (KREW).

## How to use it

1. Open the site — you land on the **home page**.
2. Click **Open the dashboard** to enter the platform.
3. Use the left-hand menu to move between views — Executive Summary, the three Scopes,
   Trend Analysis, Departments, Commuting, Buildings & Labs, and the Transition Pathway.
   The logo or the **Home** button returns you to the landing page at any time.

Works in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop or laptop.
A light/dark theme toggle sits in the top-right. An internet connection is needed so the
charts, fonts and College logo load correctly.

## The College of Engineering baseline (2025)

| Result | What it means |
|--------|----------------|
| **1,927 tCO₂e** | Total footprint across Scopes 1, 2 and 3 — the College's first complete baseline. |
| **46.5%** | Share sitting in Scope 3 — led by staff and student commuting, then the procurement supply chain. |
| **495 · 536 · 896** | tCO₂e for Scope 1 · Scope 2 · Scope 3 respectively. |
| **2040** | Net-zero target — a path of ≈128 tCO₂e/yr, led by e-shuttles, campus solar and refrigerant management. |

Coverage: eleven buildings (device-level audit), 1,115 students surveyed, a three-year
supply-chain trend (2023–2025). Methane and other gases are expressed as CO₂-equivalent.

## Who built it

KNUSTrack was developed by the **Net-Zero Carbon Emission Lab (NCEL)** at the **College of
Engineering, Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST)**. It can be updated
with new records at any time and is built to scale from the College to the wider University.
