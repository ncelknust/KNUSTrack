Reduction Opportunities
⚡ Refrigerant Management
AC refrigerant leakage & lab fugitives = 58% of Scope 1. Low-GWP refrigerant retrofits + preventive maintenance target ~150 tCO₂e/yr reduction.

97 tCO₂e from diesel generators. Solar + battery storage at NCEL displaces ~40% generator runtime within 2 years.

Transport Challenges & Decarbonisation Opportunities
🚦 Long Waiting Times
Most-cited challenge for shuttle & trotro users — especially Brunei Complex residents (1.6 km, high ridership).
💰 High Transport Cost
Bolt/Uber users at >2 km report high cost as primary barrier to low-carbon choices.
🚌 Unreliable Transit
Lack of reliable trotro & shuttle schedules affects uptake. Fixed-route e-shuttle could improve reliability.
🏙️ Traffic Congestion
Engineering gate & Kumasi city traffic a key issue. Dedicated cycle & pedestrian paths can help.
🚶 Low-Carbon Walking Majority
~58% of students walk — a strong foundation. Student hostels within 1.5 km supports this naturally.
🌱 E-Shuttle Potential
Electric shuttle replacing trotro & private cars for 3–10 km distances could save ~100 tCO₂e/yr from student commuting alone.

Priority Interventions
1. E-Shuttle & Cycle Infrastructure
Replace trotro & private car commuters with electric shuttles + cycling. Potential: ~280 tCO₂e/yr (Scope 3).
2. Campus Solar PV + Battery
Displace ECG grid electricity & diesel generators. Target: ~400 tCO₂e/yr (Scope 1+2).
3. Refrigerant Management
Low-GWP AC refrigerant retrofits across all buildings. Target: ~150 tCO₂e/yr (Scope 1 fugitive).

KREW Offset Potential
🪨 Enhanced Rock Weathering
KNUST Rock Enhanced Weathering (KREW) applies silicate rock dust to agricultural soils across Ghana. CDR potential: 50–200 tCO₂e/yr per deployment site.
📊 MRV Framework
NCEL developing rigorous Measurement, Reporting & Verification protocols for ERW carbon credits. Supports future carbon market participation.
