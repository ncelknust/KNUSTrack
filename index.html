The platform

The whole campus footprint, in one place

A complete, auditable greenhouse-gas inventory for the College of Engineering — built from procurement records, a device-level energy audit and a campus commuting survey, and presented as an interactive dashboard.

Executive summary

The full 1,927 tCO₂e footprint at a glance — every scope, its share, and the key insights driving the numbers.

Scope 1, 2 & 3 inventory

Direct emissions, purchased electricity and indirect emissions, each broken down to its underlying sources with clear methodology.

Buildings & laboratories

A device-level energy audit across eleven buildings, ranking facilities from the Petroleum Building down to the smallest block.

Commuting analytics

Employee and student travel modelled from a survey of 1,115 students — mode share, distance bands and decarbonisation options.

Three-year trends

Supply-chain emissions across 2023–2025 under three emission-factor methods (EEIO, DEFRA and Hybrid) for full transparency.

Net-zero pathway

A costed trajectory to 2040 — priority interventions plus residual-emission offsets through Enhanced Rock Weathering (KREW).

How it works

From campus records to a credible baseline

The inventory follows the GHG Protocol and rests on data the College already keeps — no new instrumentation is required to begin.

Activity data in

Procurement spend, metered electricity, fleet and generator fuel, refrigerant records and a campus commuting survey.

Emission-factor methods

Spend converted with EEIO, DEFRA and Hybrid factors; a device-level energy audit; and a distance-based commuting model.

Decisions out

Scope and department hotspots, building rankings, three-year trends and a net-zero pathway — all interactive and re-runnable.
